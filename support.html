Frequently Asked Questions

How does BookTag work offline?

BookTag features a local-first SQLite database architecture. You can log reading sessions, record quotes, and update page progress without an internet connection. Once your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or cellular data, changes automatically sync with your private cloud backup.

How do I delete my account and data?

In compliance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), account deletion is instant and fully self-serve. Open BookTag, navigate to Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account. Confirming will permanently delete your authentication record, reading history, notes, and local database immediately.

Does BookTag require sensitive device permissions?

No. BookTag is built with privacy at its core. It does not request or require access to your device camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, or GPS location.

How do the Blossom Growth Milestones work?

As you read and increment your page progress, your book cover visualizes your journey through 11 distinct Japanese cherry blossom lifecycle artwork stages — from a bare winter branch to full blossom canopy as you near completion.

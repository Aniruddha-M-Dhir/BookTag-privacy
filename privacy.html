Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 • Last Revised: November 2026

BookTag (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and safeguard your information when you use our reading companion mobile application in accordance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines and global privacy laws.

Our Privacy Promise: BookTag was built as a private sanctuary for readers. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not serve third-party advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver your personalized reading sanctuary and synchronize your reading progress across devices:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you sign in to BookTag, we collect your name and email address to authenticate your session via passwordless magic verification codes or Apple Sign-In.
  • Reading Records & User Content: Your personal reading lists (Currently Reading, Want to Read, and Completed Books), page progress, reading goals, chapter logs, completion dates, and your private notes, quotes, and reflections.
  • No Sensitive Device Permissions: BookTag operates with zero invasive permissions. We do not request or require access to your device camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or GPS location.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Minimal, non-identifying diagnostic information (such as operating system version, app version, and crash logs) strictly to maintain application stability.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for core app functionality:

  • To manage your book catalog, track reading milestones, calculate page counts, and render your visual blossom growth lifecycle.
  • To securely synchronize your library across your authorized devices via our encrypted cloud backend.
  • To authenticate your identity and deliver security-related transactional emails (e.g. login verification codes).
  • To lookup public book bibliographic metadata (such as book titles, author names, and published covers) when you search for a book.

No Sale or Marketing of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information, reading habits, notes, or contact details to data brokers, ad networks, or marketing partners.

3. Architecture, Storage & Security

BookTag is architected with an offline-first philosophy:

  • Local Storage: Your library and notes are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database, allowing full access even without an active internet connection.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When connected, your data is securely synchronized via HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections to our managed backend powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security). Each user’s data is isolated so only the authenticated user can access their records.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with a select few trusted infrastructure providers to power core functionality under strict data protection standards:

  • Supabase: For database hosting, authentication, and secure data synchronization.
  • Public Book Catalogs (Google Books API & Open Library): When you search for a book, queries containing only the search term are sent to retrieve public book metadata. No personal user identifiers or reading lists are ever transmitted to these services.
  • Apple Sign-In: Available for users choosing Apple’s privacy-preserving authentication service.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You maintain full sovereignty over your data at all times. In strict adherence to Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), BookTag provides an instant, self-serve account deletion mechanism inside the app:

To delete your account and all data:
Open BookTag → Tap Profile → Tap Delete Account → Confirm your deletion.

Upon confirmation, your user profile, authentication credentials, book library, notes, reflections, and cloud records are immediately and permanently purged from our servers and deleted from local device storage.

Alternatively, you may request permanent deletion by emailing our support desk at verify@aniruddhamdhir.dev from the email associated with your account.

6. Children’s Privacy

BookTag is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area / UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete that information.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have legal rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access and receive an export of your personal data.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate records.
  • The right to erasure (the “right to be forgotten”).
  • The right to restrict or object to certain processing.
  • The right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

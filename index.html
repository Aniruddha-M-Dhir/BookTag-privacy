Why BookTag

A Thoughtful Antidote to Modern Reading Apps

We stripped away the noise of social feeds and streaks to solve three core reading problems.

🌿

Quiet Mindful Pacing

Traditional apps use push notifications and broken streak penalties that induce guilt. BookTag respects your tempo with an organic cherry blossom tree that flourishes naturally with each page you read.

📖

Chapter-Level Retention

Skimming 50 books a year is pointless if you forget them all in a month. BookTag lets you break books down into discrete chapters to capture summaries, key learnings, and personal reflections as you progress.

🔒

Total Data Sovereignty

Never lose your private thoughts to poor subway connectivity or server outages. BookTag writes to an on-device SQLite database first, then quietly backs up to your private cloud storage when you reconnect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about compatibility, privacy, and how BookTag works.

Yes, 100%. BookTag is engineered with an offline-first architecture. All books, chapter milestones, reading sessions, and notes are stored locally inside SQLite on your device first. You can read and write notes on flights, subways, or in the woods with zero network signal.
Whenever your phone is connected to the internet, BookTag silently synchronizes all your local records with your secure Supabase cloud vault. If you get a new iPhone or open BookTag on your iPad, your entire library, reading progress, and notes are restored automatically.
Each book features a dynamic Sakura blossom tree that evolves in real time based on your reading progress percentage. Starting as a delicate green shoot at 0%, it sprouts leafy branches at 25%, blooms with tender pink buds at 50%, and flourishes into full cherry blossom flowers when you reach the final pages.
Yes, strictly private. We do not sell data to advertisers, use tracking cookies, or analyze the contents of your reading reflections. PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) guarantees that only your authenticated account can ever read or modify your library.
BookTag features full self-serve data deletion compliant with Apple Guideline 5.1.1. Simply open the app, head to Profile → Delete Account, and tap confirm. This immediately erases your local SQLite database and permanently wipes all cloud records.
BookTag is currently available for iOS devices running iOS 15.0 or later, including all modern iPhones and iPads.
